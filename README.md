## How do trading fees compare across major cryptocurrency exchanges?

Kraken offers some of the lowest fees starting at 0.16% maker and 0.26% taker, decreasing to 0% for high-volume traders, while Coinbase charges 0.40%-0.60% on Advanced Trade and up to 4% on Simple Trade depending on payment methods. Volume-based discounts reward active traders across most platforms, with monthly trading amounts determining fee tiers.

Bybit implements innovative negative maker fees at -0.025%, actually paying liquidity providers while charging 0.075% for takers. PrimeXBT delivers ultra-low rates at 0.01% maker and 0.02% taker for derivatives. Exchange-specific tokens sometimes provide additional fee reductions when used for payment.

## What security measures should I look for in a cryptocurrency exchange?

Cold storage architecture represents the foundation of exchange security, with leading platforms storing 90-95% of customer funds offline in multi-signature wallets. Proof-of-reserves audits demonstrate that exchanges maintain full backing of customer deposits through transparent verification. Two-factor authentication, biometric login, hardware security modules, and withdrawal address whitelisting provide account-level protection.

Insurance coverage varies dramatically between platforms. Some exchanges maintain crime insurance for hot wallet breaches while others offer no coverage, with cryptocurrency holdings typically not FDIC-insured unlike fiat balances. Regulatory compliance under frameworks like New York's BitLicense or EU licensing requirements adds oversight layers that unregulated exchanges lack.
